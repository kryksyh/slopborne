Third-party components in Slopborne.app (macOS)

Bundled libraries (Slopborne.app/Contents/Frameworks):
- MoltenVK (Khronos Group), Apache-2.0: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/MoltenVK
- SDL3, zlib license: https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL
- FFmpeg (libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswresample, libswscale), LGPL-2.1-or-later:
  https://ffmpeg.org (source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html)
- OpenSSL (libssl, libcrypto), Apache-2.0: https://www.openssl.org
- miniupnpc, BSD-3-Clause: https://miniupnp.tuxfamily.org
- xxHash, BSD-2-Clause: https://github.com/Cyan4973/xxHash
- epoll-shim, MIT: https://github.com/jiixyj/epoll-shim
- fmt, MIT: https://github.com/fmtlib/fmt
- Zydis and Zycore, MIT: https://github.com/zyantific/zydis

Compiled in: shadPS4 (GPL-2.0-or-later), sirit, Dear ImGui, magic_enum, miniz, half, xbyak,
cpp-httplib, wepoll, nlohmann/json, robin-map, Vulkan Memory Allocator, LibAtrac9, FSR-Vulkan and
the AMD FidelityFX SDK (FSR 1 and FSR 3.1), DejaVu fonts. Their license texts are in this folder
where the source tree carries one.

Picture filters: shaders from libretro/glsl-shaders, converted to compute shaders; authors and
licenses per shader in retro-filters-ATTRIBUTION.md.

Apple MetalFX is part of macOS and is not redistributed.
